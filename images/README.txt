HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTO TO THE VALENTINE'S WEBSITE

1. Place your photo in this "images" folder
2. Rename your photo to something simple like "valentine.jpg" or "us.png"
3. Edit the index.html file and update the image src to point to your photo
   Change this line:
   <img class="art" src="https://placehold.co/400x300/ffd6e7/ff3b7a?text=Your+Photo+Here" alt="Valentine's Photo" />
   
   To this (replace with your actual filename):
   <img class="art" src="images/valentine.jpg" alt="Valentine's Photo" />

TIPS FOR CHOOSING A GOOD PHOTO:
- Choose a photo with both of you together
- Square or landscape orientation works best
- Higher resolution is better for clarity
- Photos with good lighting will look best
- Consider a romantic or meaningful photo that has special significance
